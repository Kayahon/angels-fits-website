# Angel's Fits: scroll-craft brief

Interviewed (2026-11-16 and 2026-11-20). Answers below are the user's own choices; where they picked from offered options, the option text is quoted. Business facts come from content/Content.docx and the user's earlier answers in website-plan.md. Nothing invented.

## The eight topics

1. **Vibe:** "Coachella theme, modern". Colors: "Earthy desert tones". Style range: "Editorial" (like a festival fashion magazine: big type, photos as spreads).
2. **Scroll journey (their sequence):** Home, Outfits, How It Works, Book (from the brief). Pages become scenes on one page.
3. **Energy curve (authored, from their picks):** calm open, building to the peak, lively range, quiet practical close.
4. **Feeling and the ONE moment:** "Building a look: as you scroll, a full outfit assembles piece by piece: hat, top, skirt, belt, boots. Shows the mix-and-match idea."
5. **Something no other site does:** seeded by the peak and by the brand's real offer ("Pick a look. Or make your own." / mix-and-match). Authored: the hang tag (below).
6. **Range from premium-minimal:** "Editorial".
7. **One world or scenes:** "Separate scenes".
8. **Assets:** "Only my photos". 7 outfit photos (mannequin, grey backdrop), 3 kids' portraits ("Kids' outfits we rent"), logo. No AI generation, no spend.

## Hard brand rules
- Real business: no invented prices, reviews, numbers, sizes, addresses. "Message us for rates".
- Contact: Facebook "Angel's Outfit Rental" (exact URL still a placeholder), replies 8am to 8pm. Mactan, Lapu-Lapu City. No street address.
- Facts: online only, no physical store; fittings by appointment at LG Garden; personal pickup or Maxim delivery; 3-day rental (pickup, event, return); late returns add a daily fee depending on the item. Party outfits too, not only cowgirl. Kids' outfits too.
- Booking: real form, Formspree free plan, to the owner's email (endpoint not created yet; form says so honestly until it is).
- User explicitly asked earlier for a subtle trailing cursor: kept (desktop only), overriding the taste refuse-list default because the brief earned it.

## Journey
1. Recognition: that is the outfit I want for my event (hero look, labelled)
2. Curiosity: what is in the wardrobe (quiet index)
3. Delight (PEAK): a look gets dressed piece by piece
4. Appetite: the range of looks, each one taggable
5. Warmth: kids' outfits too
6. Ease: how 3-day renting works
7. Resolve: book, with the tagged look already filled in

## Feeling curve
1. Recognition: Look 05 standing in an arch, planes drifting apart, a factual label under it
2. Calm: an editorial wardrobe index on sand, small and still (authored quiet before the peak)
3. Delight: grey ghost mannequin, then hat, corset, skirt and belt, boots arrive in colour one by one while the hang tag ticks each piece
4. Appetite: five more looks travel sideways, each with "Tag this look"
5. Warmth: a wipe opens onto three kids' portraits
6. Ease: dark band, three days laid out plainly, the practical facts
7. Resolve: the booking form, holding, with the tag's look pre-selected

## The peak
"I scrolled and it dressed the mannequin one piece at a time, hat first, boots last." Act 3, largest span on the page.

## Tell-someone sentence
It's the site where scrolling dresses the mannequin piece by piece, and the hang tag you fill in comes with you to the booking form.

## Authored silence
Act 2 is deliberately quiet (small type, no motion beyond entrance) so the peak has something to arrive from. Not dead scroll.
