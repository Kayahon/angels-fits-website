How it works

Pickup, party, return.

All online

We're an online shop with no physical store, so everything is arranged by appointment. Here's how a rental goes, start to finish.

01 / Before your event

Three easy steps.

Plan ahead: fittings and pickups are set up in advance.

01

Reserve your look

Fill in the booking form with your event date and the outfit you like, or send us a message on Facebook. We'll reply with availability and rates.

02

Come for a fitting

Try it on before the big day. Fittings are on a meet-up basis at LG Garden, by appointment only.

03

Collect it

Pick up your outfit in person, or get it delivered by Maxim.

02 / The rental period

Every rental is 3 days.

One day to collect, one day to shine, one day to bring it back.

01

Outfit pickup

Collect in person or by Maxim delivery.

02

Event day

Wear it, love it, take the photos.

03

Outfit return

Bring it back so the next cowgirl can wear it.

Late returns: an extra daily rental fee applies, depending on the item rented.

03 / Questions

Good to know.

Do you have a physical store?

No, we're online only. All fittings and rentals are arranged in advance through our booking form or Facebook page.

Where are fittings?

We meet at LG Garden in Mactan, Lapu-Lapu City, by appointment.

Can you deliver my outfit?

Yes. You can pick it up in person or have it delivered by Maxim.

How much does a rental cost?

Rates depend on the outfit. Send us a booking request or a message and we'll tell you the rate for the look you like.

What if I return it late?

Late returns add an extra daily rental fee, depending on the item rented.

When can I reach you?

We're available 8am to 8pm. Message us on Facebook or use the booking form any time.
